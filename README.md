# Law of Chaos

**Article 1.** Chaos is not the absence of law but the presence of too many
laws enforced at once. Enforcement capacity is therefore rationed.

**Article 2.** The butterfly effect is acknowledged: small commits may
produce large, unpredictable consequences. Commit accordingly.

**Article 3.** Order and chaos are not enemies but shifts; the Departement
permits both on alternating days.
